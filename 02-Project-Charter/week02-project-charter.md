## Business Need
RCHN is looking to replace its aging patient intake and scheduling system across six clinics. The current system has caused delays in their workflow. Addressing this need is vital to the progression of RCHN'S clinics. this project will allow for better communication and alignment between internal staff and patients.

---
## Purpose and High-Level Scope
The purpose of this project is to replace and enhance the workflow of RCHN’s intake and scheduling system across six clinics. This will include a more reliable platform for staff who use the system, better alignment between clinic workflows and organizational needs, training for employees, and support for patients and their needs.

---
## Success Criteria
Some observable outcomes that will determine the success of this project include data showing decreased patient wait times, the ability of staff to adapt to the new system, and the operational upkeep of the system.
